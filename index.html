Selected work

Bachelor thesis, 2025

Can LLMs fool deception classifiers?

I built an attack pipeline where six open-source LLMs paraphrase stories until a DistilBERT deception classifier flips its verdict, without changing the meaning. 3,600 attacks across three prompting strategies.

Clean accuracy
78%
Under attack
42%

GPT-OSS 120B succeeded 70% of the time in about two tries, pushing AUC from 0.85 to chance. Lies were far easier to pass off as truth than the reverse.

DistilBERT, Llama 3.3, GPT-OSS, Sentence-BERT, statistical testing. Code

2025

Coral bleaching detection

SegFormer model that measures bleached coral in cm² from reef photos. Area estimates were off by 47 cm² on average on a 2,500 cm² quadrat, with R² 0.97 against hand-measured ground truth and a 6.7-point error on bleaching ratio.

SegFormer, PyTorch, Dice + Focal loss
