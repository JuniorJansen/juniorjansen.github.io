Selected work

proj_01adversarial nlp · thesis · 2025

Adversarial attacks on deception classifiers

My bachelor thesis. I tested whether LLMs can trick a RoBERTa-based deception classifier by paraphrasing lies until they slip past it. Built the attack pipeline myself and measured success rates, confidence drops, and how many attempts each model needed. Turns out: relevant if you care about misinformation detection.

RoBERTaTransformersLLMsPython
proj_02healthcare robotics · 2025-2026

ElderCareAI: robotic stroke rehabilitation

A robot arm that coaches stroke patients through physio exercises at home. It watches the movement, catches errors, and corrects them on the spot, with an interface that walks patients through each exercise and scores their progress.

RoboticsImitation learningPyTorchReal-time CV
proj_03reinforcement learning · 2024-25

Automated play: RL for Mario Party Advance

We taught RL agents to play 50+ Mario Party Advance mini-games with Stable Retro and Sample Factory, hitting 3000+ FPS in training. My part: writing reward functions, digging game state out of RAM, and tuning per game so multi-task training didn't fall apart.

Stable RetroSample FactoryMulti-agent RL
proj_04remote sensing · 2025

Coral bleaching detection

Trained a SegFormer model to spot coral bleaching in underwater reef photos. Added pixel-to-centimeter conversion so the output is actual bleached surface area, not just pretty masks, and wrapped it in an inference pipeline that scales to full reef surveys.

SegFormerSegmentationPyTorch
proj_05predictive modelling · 2024

Residential burglary risk in London

Forecasting burglary risk per London neighbourhood (LSOA level) from socio-economic and mobility data, plus features we engineered ourselves, like a digital exclusion index. The models fed into a plan for where police should put their resources, all served up in a Streamlit dashboard.

StreamlitScikit-LearnGeospatialFeature engineering
